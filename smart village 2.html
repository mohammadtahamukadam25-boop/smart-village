About Project

Empowering Villages With Technology

The Smart Village IT Enablement Project aims to reduce the digital divide by making useful digital information easier to understand and discover.

    Digital Safety

    Stay Safe Online

    Simple digital-safety habits can protect you and your family.

    ✅ Do

    • 🔐 Use strong passwords.
    • 📱 Keep your phone and apps updated.
    • 🔎 Verify websites before entering information.
    • 💳 Check payment details before confirming.
    • 📞 Report suspicious activity through official channels.

    ❌ Don't

    • 🚫 Never share OTPs or PINs.
    • 🚫 Don't click suspicious links.
    • 🚫 Don't trust random job offers asking for money.
    • 🚫 Don't share passwords.
    • 🚫 Don't install unknown applications.
    🔐 Report online financial/cyber fraudNational Cyber Crime Portal ↗
